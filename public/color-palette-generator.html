HELPFUL NOTES

Frequently asked questions

Are the palettes guaranteed to be accessible?

No. Color harmony and contrast accessibility are different. Check specific foreground/background combinations before using colors for body text, buttons or critical UI.

Can I enter RGB or HSL?

This version accepts six-digit HEX values. Convert colors with the Color Code Converter in the free tools directory.

Is a palette uploaded or saved?

No. The palette is generated locally in your browser and is not stored by this tool.
