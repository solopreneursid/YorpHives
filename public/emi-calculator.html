Loan EMI Calculator

Estimate the monthly instalment for a fixed-rate loan, along with total repayment and interest across the selected tenure.

How EMI is estimated

For a fixed monthly interest rate, the standard EMI formula estimates a constant monthly payment across the loan term. The result changes with principal, rate and tenure.

This is a planning estimate and may exclude processing fees, insurance, rate resets and lender-specific rules. It is not a loan offer or financial advice. See more YorpHives calculators.
