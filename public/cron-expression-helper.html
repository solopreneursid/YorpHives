HELPFUL NOTES

Frequently asked questions

What does */15 9-17 * * 1-5 mean?

It describes every 15 minutes during hours 9 through 17 on weekdays, using common five-field Unix-style syntax. Confirm inclusive hour and weekday behaviour in your scheduler.

Does this calculate the next scheduled run?

No. It validates common field syntax and describes the fields; it does not calculate exact future run dates or times.

Do all cron schedulers use the same syntax?

No. Some schedulers support extra fields or different rules. Treat this as a helper for common five-field syntax and check your scheduler’s own documentation.
