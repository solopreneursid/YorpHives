QUICK ANSWERS

Product availability & access.

Clear expectations about what is available today and how original products are presented.

Are products separate from Free Tools?

Yes. Products lists YorpHives-built apps, extensions and workflow products. The Free Tools directory is reserved for individual utilities that are genuinely free to use.

Which products are available now?

Availability is shown on each product or its destination page. QuickPanel, Workbench and the Local Productivity Suite are usable now; browser extension packages require manual installation where stated. Planned items are labelled rather than presented as published products.

Are all products free?

Current free access is stated where applicable. Future paid products or paid features will need their own clear pricing and access terms; no unannounced price is implied here.

Are the listed agents autonomous AI agents?

No. The current Workbench assistants are user-invoked, rule-based helpers and workflows. They do not independently browse the web or run as autonomous cloud agents.

Which original plugins are available?

The Local Productivity Suite is available as a free manual-install Chromium extension. It combines text cleanup, explicitly saved snippets and local page notes. More independent plugins will be listed after their working packages and permission/data policies are ready.
