PRODUCT QUALITY / PUBLISHING STANDARD

Release responsibly.
Keep every claim verifiable.

This checklist is a repeatable pre-release workflow for YorpHives products. It is not proof that every existing package has passed every test. Product records separately state their lifecycle and whether the latest release has been independently verified.

Lifecycle states

PlannedNot released. Do not imply that it can be used now.
TestingEvaluation build; limitations and testing scope must be clear.
AvailablePublished for intended use, with access and compatibility stated.
MaintenanceExisting users may continue, but active development or new adoption is limited.
RetiredNo longer recommended for new use; point to a replacement or archive where possible.

Catalogue rules

  • Each product needs a unique ID and a valid category reference.
  • Product destinations must be same-site paths; internal page anchors must exist.
  • Lifecycle, access terms, compatibility, installation and permission notes must be explicit.
  • Do not invent release dates or claim verification that has not been performed.
  • Keep Free Tools separate from products that may later have paid access.
