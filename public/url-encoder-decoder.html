URL Encoder & Decoder

Percent-encode a string for use as a URL component, or decode an encoded component back into readable text. Handy for query parameters, debugging and web development.

When should you encode a URL?

URL component encoding replaces characters that have special meaning or are not safe in a component with percent-encoded representations. Encode individual values rather than blindly encoding an entire URL, because separators such as & and ? have structural meaning.
