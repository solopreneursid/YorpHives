GST Calculator India

Quickly work out GST on a price or extract the tax component from a GST-inclusive total. Choose a common rate and get a clear base-price, tax and total breakdown.

How GST calculation works

To add GST, multiply the base price by the selected rate and add that tax to the base. To extract GST from a tax-inclusive price, divide the total by 1 plus the rate, then subtract the base from the total.

Common GST slabs include 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%, but the applicable rate depends on the specific goods or services. This calculator is an estimate, not tax advice.
