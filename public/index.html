FAQ / HOW WE WORK

Technical details.
No vague promises.

How the tools are delivered, what runs locally, and what to expect when commissioning a build.

What can I use YorpHives for?

YorpHives combines free browser utilities, original browser and web tools, and project-based engineering services covering websites, applications, integrations, and practical automation.

Are YorpHives’s online tools free?

The Free Tools directory is reserved for tools currently offered at no charge. Products and future paid features are kept separate and should show their access terms clearly.

Do the tools upload my text or files?

Many utilities process text and data locally in your browser. Check each tool’s instructions for its specific behaviour; PDF and image tools may load browser libraries from a content delivery network and may need an internet connection.

What is the YorpHives Workbench?

The Workbench groups focused utilities for text handling, saved snippets, notes, research capture, job tracking, quotation building, and repeatable tasks. It is a tool workspace, not a claim that every module is AI-powered or cloud-autonomous.

What is the QuickPanel browser extension?

The YorpHives QuickPanel extension is an original browser companion for selected-text actions, page capture, table extraction, saved snippets and user-invoked form assistance. It is currently distributed as a manual-install prototype.

Can YorpHives help with a website or business automation project?

Yes. Use the project enquiry form to describe the goal, requirements, rough budget and timeline. YorpHives reviews requests and discusses an appropriate next step.

Can students get help with technical projects?

YorpHives can provide learning-focused guidance, debugging, explanations and deployment support so students understand and complete their work honestly.
