HELPFUL NOTES

Frequently asked questions

What does the contrast ratio mean?

It compares the relative luminance of the selected foreground and background colors. Higher ratios generally make text easier to distinguish.

Does a passing ratio mean my page is fully accessible?

No. This tool checks a color pair only. Accessibility also depends on font size, states, focus indicators, non-text controls and other page features.

Does it support shorthand HEX colors?

This version expects six-digit HEX values, such as #111827 or #ffffff.
