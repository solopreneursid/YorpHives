Early-access transparency
LifeOS currently stores its workspace in browser local storage on this device. There are no LifeOS accounts or cloud sync, encrypted server-side storage, file uploads, connected AI planner, bank integrations, or browser notifications. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access its data. Do not store passwords, financial account credentials, medical records, or other highly sensitive information here.
