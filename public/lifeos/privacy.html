Privacy & data in LifeOS

This notice describes the current LifeOS early-access prototype, not future features that may be built later.

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Product status: early-access prototype

In short: LifeOS currently saves your workspace in local storage in the browser profile you use. The current prototype does not send your tasks, habits, goals, expense entries or notes to a LifeOS cloud account because accounts and cloud sync are not implemented.

What data can you enter?

You may enter task and reminder titles, categories, due dates and priorities; habit names and check-ins; goal titles, progress and milestones; manually entered expense or subscription amounts; study/career items; and document reference titles. The prototype also keeps timestamps and app state needed to display and back up your workspace.

Where is it stored?

Workspace data is stored in your browser's local storage on the current device and browser profile. It is not encrypted by LifeOS. Other people who can use the same unlocked device or browser profile may be able to see or change it. Clearing site data, changing browser profiles or using a different device can make the workspace unavailable unless you have a backup.

What is not connected?

  • No LifeOS account registration or cloud synchronization.
  • No bank or payment-account connection.
  • No file uploads or secure document vault; the Documents area stores references/titles only.
  • No connected AI daily planner; the focus list is generated locally from open tasks.
  • No browser notification integration or billing feature.

Backups and imports

You can export a JSON backup from the app. That file may contain personal information you entered, so store it carefully and do not share it publicly. Importing a backup replaces the current local workspace after confirmation. Keep a separate backup before importing if you need to preserve the existing data.

How can you clear your data?

Use the app's Reset workspace option to replace the current workspace with starter sample data. You can also clear site data for the LifeOS origin in your browser settings. These actions may be difficult or impossible to undo without a backup.

Installable app and offline use

The current prototype can be installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA) in compatible browsers. Its service worker caches the app shell while workspace data remains in local browser storage. This is not a native APK or app-store package. The planned product direction is a cross-platform native-capable app; native builds are not yet available. Installation and offline support for each future platform must be verified on target devices.

Future changes

Accounts, cloud synchronization, server-side storage, AI features or additional integrations would change how data is processed. They are not active in this prototype and should be accompanied by an updated privacy notice and clear choices before launch.
