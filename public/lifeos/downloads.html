SIMPLE INSTALLATION IS THE GOAL

Download. Install. Get started.

For supported native platforms, the intended release experience is one clear download action for the right package, followed by normal operating-system installation. We will not label a package “ready” until install, upgrade, offline launch, data persistence and backup/restore checks pass on the target platform.

01 · GET THE APPChoose your operating system and download its verified release package.
02 · INSTALLFollow the OS's installation prompt; permissions remain under your control.
03 · START LOCALUse LifeOS locally first. Optional sync, if added later, will be explicit and opt-in.
