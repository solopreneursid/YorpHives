Discount Calculator

Work out how much you save during a sale, calculate the price after a percentage discount and optionally estimate tax on the discounted price.

How to calculate a discount

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage to find the amount saved, then subtract that saving from the original price. The optional tax field estimates tax on the discounted amount; actual taxes and store rules may differ.
