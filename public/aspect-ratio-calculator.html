Aspect Ratio Calculator

Find the resized height for a new width while keeping the original proportions of an image, video frame or design canvas.

Preserve image proportions

To preserve the aspect ratio, multiply the new width by the original height divided by the original width. The calculated height may be fractional; round to the nearest pixel when your workflow requires whole-number dimensions.
