HELPFUL NOTES

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. The page reads the selected image in your browser using a local object URL. It does not send the image to a YorpHives server.

Does it resize or compress the image?

No. This tool reports metadata only. Use the separate PDF and image tools to resize or convert supported images.

What does aspect ratio mean?

It is the width divided by the height. It helps compare image proportions independent of their actual pixel size.
