Unix Timestamp Converter

Convert Unix timestamps to human-readable dates, or turn a date and time into Unix seconds and milliseconds for debugging APIs and logs.

What is a Unix timestamp?

A Unix timestamp counts elapsed seconds since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC; some systems use milliseconds instead. This tool estimates the unit by magnitude by default, and also lets you choose seconds or milliseconds explicitly for older timestamps whose units are ambiguous. It shows UTC and local representations.
